Continuity. Honesty. Your agency.

The way we're building

Personal, with principles.

Ioleia is being designed to grow more useful with context and time.
These are the commitments guiding that work.

01 /

Continuity that matters.

Carry meaningful context forward, so each conversation can build on what came before. Personalization should come from what you choose to share.

02 /

Honesty, always.

Be clear about uncertainty, acknowledge mistakes, and stay truthful about being AI. A personal connection should never depend on pretending.

03 /

You remain in charge.

Support your thinking and your choices. Respect your boundaries, your relationships, and the life you lead beyond the conversation.

04 /

Privacy, built into the work.

Privacy and security are engineering requirements. We are testing and qualifying those protections before inviting people into the private Alpha.
