About Ioleia

Ioleia Inc. is a Canadian technology company building an AI companion intended to develop meaningful continuity with the person using it while remaining honest about what it is and careful with human autonomy.

The company is currently focused on preparing its first private Alpha and the security, privacy, identity, and reliability work required before real users are admitted.

What we are building

Ioleia is being designed as a personal AI companion that can remember relevant context, understand the meaning behind a conversation, and build continuity over time.

The private Alpha will be invitation-only. Ioleia is not currently offering public account creation, public subscriptions, or general consumer access.
