What we're building

Not just another chat window.

Ioleia is being built as a personal AI companion with continuity. The goal is not a string of disconnected chats, but an experience that can understand the meaning behind a conversation, remember relevant context, and become more useful and personal over time.

The product is being developed for iPhone first. Early real-user testing has already informed the experience, and a small invitation-only private Alpha is now being prepared.

Continuity

Remembering what matters and carrying useful context forward instead of starting from zero every time.

Understanding

Working with conversation, memory, and images in context rather than treating each input as an isolated prompt.

Relationship

Designed to feel increasingly personal without pretending to be human or taking control away from the person using it.

Principles

The relationship matters. So do the boundaries.

Truthful by design

Ioleia should not invent certainty, manipulate a person, or misrepresent what it is.

Human agency matters

The person remains in charge of their choices, their information, and their relationship with the product.

Continuity should be earned

Personalization should come from real, permitted context and memory — not from pretending a relationship exists when it does not.

Privacy and security are release requirements, not marketing decoration. Ioleia is qualifying those systems before admitting real Alpha users.

Current stage

Preparing the first private Alpha.

Ioleia is not open for public account creation yet. Live access is intentionally limited while authentication, privacy, security, and production AI routing are being qualified for the real Alpha path.
