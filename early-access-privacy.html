Early Access Privacy

Draft notice for the planned register. Signups are not live; no email addresses are being collected through this form.

Ioleia Inc. uses the email you submit to provide launch and availability updates about Ioleia.

Your email is encrypted and stored in a private register in AWS’s Canada Central region. The register also holds a lookup code, the time you joined, your subscription status, the signup source, and the version of the consent statement you saw. We do not sell your email.

The signup form does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage. We do not add your IP address, browser information, or browsing activity to the register.

You can unsubscribe, withdraw consent, ask to have your email removed, or ask a privacy question by emailing heather@ioleia.com.

This notice covers the early-access register only. Joining does not create an Ioleia product account.
